# Clinical Guideline Sources

The retrieval corpus was created from the following WHO publications:

1. WHO. Guideline for the pharmacological treatment of hypertension in adults (2021).
2. WHO. Prevention and Control of Noncommunicable Diseases: Guidelines for primary health care in low-resource settings.
3. WHO HEARTS. Technical package for cardiovascular disease management in primary health care: Evidence-based treatment protocols (2018).

Source documents are licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO.

The text was extracted, cleaned, chunked and indexed for
academic research and retrieval experiments.

should be consulted when redistributing or adapting this material.
